Before you upload

Good verification starts with careful handling.

  • Keep the original file if you can. Screenshots and re-uploads lose metadata.
  • Note where you first saw the media, not only where you found it later.
  • Record the date claimed by the source. We compare it against reverse search results.
Privacy: Do not upload private or sensitive media of real people without a good reason. If you run the AI analysis, the image is sent to Google's Gemini service.
