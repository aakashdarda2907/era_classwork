STEP-BY-STEP GUIDE

Verify Media in Four Steps

Combine these checks to make a more informed assessment.

01
🔍

Check the Source

Find the original publisher, website, or account. Check the publication date, location, author, and context. Be cautious of anonymous sources and sensational claims.

Ask: Who published this, and why?
02
🗃

Check Metadata

Examine available file details such as creation date, dimensions, file type, and camera information. Remember that metadata may be removed or changed.

Ask: Do the file details make sense?
03
🖼

Reverse Image Search

Search an image or a video keyframe to find similar images and earlier appearances online. Compare dates, locations, captions, and original versions.

Ask: Has this appeared somewhere else?
04
📰

Cross-Check Reports

Look for independent reporting, credible news coverage, official statements, and reliable eyewitness accounts. Check whether the sources confirm the same claim.

Ask: Can independent sources confirm it?

RESPONSIBLE VERIFICATION

Limitations and Ethics

No verification tool can provide absolute proof that an image or video is authentic or manipulated. Search results may be incomplete, metadata may be missing, and AI-generated media can be difficult to identify reliably.

  • Treat tool results as guidance, not a final verdict.
  • Use multiple independent sources to check important claims.
  • Respect privacy, consent, and the original context of media.
  • Do not accuse people of manipulation without sufficient evidence.
  • Be transparent about uncertainty when sharing conclusions.

Remember: Pause, investigate, cross-check, and then decide whether the media is trustworthy.

Think critically. Verify responsibly. Share carefully.
